10 Where the numbers land

Every link above goes through /go/<code>, which redirects to the homepage carrying UTM parameters. Umami reads them.

Posted asCodeShows up as
YouTube, main upload/go/ytyoutube / social / main-upload
YouTube Shorts/go/ytsyoutube / social / shorts
LinkedIn/go/lilinkedin / social / post
X/go/xx / social / post
TikTok bio/go/tttiktok / social / bio
Instagram bio/go/iginstagram / social / bio
Facebook/go/fbfacebook / social / post
  1. Read them in Umami under Reports, then the UTM report, filtered to campaign launch-2026.
  2. Do not rely on the referrer instead. Links opened inside the TikTok, Instagram and Facebook in-app browsers arrive with no referrer or a generic app one, and this site sends strict-origin-when-cross-origin. The UTM parameters are in the URL, so they survive all of that. That is the whole reason the codes exist.
  3. Test each code once before posting. Open it, confirm you land on the homepage with the parameters still in the address bar, and confirm the visit appears in Umami. A redirect that 404s loses the click silently.

11 Posting order

  1. LinkedIn and YouTube first. Both index and stay findable.
  2. X the same day, linking the YouTube upload in a reply.
  3. Instagram and Facebook next day, same film, softer framing.
  4. TikTok and Shorts 24–48h later. A second wave rather than one spike.
  5. Pin the shop link everywhere it is allowed.

12 Before you post

  • Watch each file to the end, with sound on
  • Confirm the vertical captions sit above the platform interface
  • Check the shop is up, shop.lebon-grace.com
  • Confirm AED 15 and the 2–3 day lead time are still true
  • Have someone ready to answer comments for the first few hours
One caveat worth holding. These films are strongest on the promise, and the promise is checkable : which is why every caption is a fact. If one stops being true (the price, the lead time, the free engraving) the films stop being usable and should come down rather than be quietly left up.
