## Which file goes where

| Platform | File | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **YouTube** (main) | `out/correction-master.mp4` — 61s, 1920×1080 | The argument film. Rewards a viewer who stays. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | `out/making-vertical.mp4` — 52s, 1080×1920 | Process content, vertical, under the Shorts limit. |
| **LinkedIn** | `out/correction-master.mp4` | Story-led and business-literate. The corrections *are* the post. |
| **X** | `out/making-master.mp4` — 52s, 1920×1080 | Plays inline in-feed; process holds attention without sound. |
| **TikTok** | `out/making-vertical.mp4` | Making-of is native to the format. |

**The vertical cuts were re-rendered for this.** TikTok and Shorts paint their
own UI over the lowest 15–20% of the frame, and the captions originally sat
inside it — invisible on both platforms. They now sit at 20% (Making) and 17%
(Correction) from the bottom, clear of it.

**All four files carry sound.** Room tone, per-shot foley, narration, and one
sustained warm note under the end card. Post with sound on.

## Posting order

1. **LinkedIn and YouTube first.** They index and stay findable.
2. **X same day**, linking the YouTube upload in a reply.
3. **TikTok and Shorts 24–48h later** — same film, different audience, and it
   gives you a second wave rather than one spike.

Pin the shop link everywhere it is allowed.

## Before you post

- [ ] Watch each file to the end with sound on
- [ ] Confirm the vertical captions are visible above the platform UI
- [ ] Check the shop is up: https://shop.lebon-grace.com
- [ ] Make sure stock levels and the AED 15 price are still true
- [ ] Have someone ready to answer comments for the first few hours

**One caveat worth holding.** These films are strongest on the promise, and the
promise is checkable — which is why every caption is a fact. If any of them
stops being true (the price, the lead time, the free engraving), the films stop
being usable and should come down rather than be quietly left up.
