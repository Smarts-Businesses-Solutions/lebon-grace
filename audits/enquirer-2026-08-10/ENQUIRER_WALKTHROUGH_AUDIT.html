Confirmed findings

HIGH · EN-01 Genuine enquiries cannot be delivered

The guide states the Resend sender domain is unverified; the route attempts mail and correctly returns 500 on failure. A customer cannot depend on the contact form.

Fix: verify sender, then use an approved owned recipient for one full delivery/reply test.

MEDIUM · EN-02 Mobile sticky header obscures focused message field

At 393×852 the measured field/header intersection was 65px (desktop: 0). This impairs message review while typing.

Fix: focus scroll offset or scroll-margin-top; regression-test no intersection.

MEDIUM · EN-03 Phone number is collected but not delivered

UI submits phone, but server destructures no phone and email body does not include it. Callback information is silently discarded.

Fix: include escaped phone with stated data purpose, or remove unused field.

MEDIUM · EN-04 Abuse throttles reset on deployment / do not share state

Contact and reveal use an in-memory Map. Restart/deploy resets it; multiple replicas have independent allowances.

Fix: durable shared/edge limit and multi-instance operational test.

LOW · EN-05 No route or production E2E test covers contact

CI runs test suites but repository inventory contains no contact/reveal route test or matching E2E.

Fix: safe mocked route + E2E coverage for error, honeypot, escape and reveal flows.
