# Lebon Grace: Pivot to Locally Made Wooden Toys

**Written 2026-08-01.** Supersedes `LEBON-GRACE-SOURCING-BLUEPRINT.md`, which described a
dropshipping business that no longer exists.

---

## 1. What changed

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Product | 515 dropshipped items from CJ (China) | 41 laser-cut wooden toys made in-house |
| Fulfilment | Air freight per order, 8 to 50 days | Cut to order in the workshop, 2 to 3 days |
| Marginal cost | Near zero, priced on volume | Your time, priced on margin |
| Delivery | AED 8.70 baked into every unit | Pickup free, local courier, international optional |
| Inventory | Supplier stock you cannot see | Made to order, no stock risk |
| Returns | Standard distance selling | Restricted, made to order, faulty replaced |

The old blueprint targeted "1,000+ SKUs at AED 5 to 25, impulse-buy territory". That was a
sound strategy for commodity goods with no marginal cost. It is the wrong strategy for
handmade products, and the AED 5 price anchor is the single most damaging thing carried
over from it.

## 3. Pricing

### The honest competitive picture

You asked whether MDF puzzles on Amazon.ae and Noon are cheap. They are. Mass-produced
imports sit roughly AED 20 to 60. I could not pull exact live figures (Noon blocks
automated access) so treat that range as indicative, not verified.

That matters because it is the number a customer sees when they search. But it is the
wrong number to price against, for one reason:

**You cannot win a price fight against a factory.** A plant running 100,000 units has a
unit cost of a few dirhams. You have laser time and your own hands. Matching their price
means working for nothing.

### Two viable strategies

**A. Compete on price.** Sell near AED 39 to 59, accept thin margin, win on "made in UAE"
sentiment alone. Works only with high volume, which made-to-order cannot deliver because
your capacity is your own hours.

**B. Refuse the comparison.** Sell something the import cannot be. This is the
recommendation.

### What makes yours a different product

1. **Personalisation.** A child's name engraved on the board. A factory in Yiwu cannot do
   this. It typically commands 2 to 3x and it is the single strongest lever you have.
2. **Made to order in the UAE.** Days, not weeks. No customs. Local accountability.
3. **Design exclusivity.** Your catalogue, not the same SKU seventy other sellers list.
4. **Gift framing.** Birthdays, Eid, new baby, nursery gifts. Gift buyers are far less
   price sensitive than self-buyers.
5. **The photography.** Already editorial grade. It earns a premium price; a cheap price
   would contradict it and read as suspicious.

### Recommended structure

| Tier | Products | Plain | Personalised |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | Stencils, simple peg puzzles | AED 49 | AED 79 |
| Core | Alphabet and number boards, animal puzzles | AED 69 | AED 99 |
| Premium | 3D puzzles, Montessori boards, multi-layer | AED 99 | AED 139 |
| Colour-in | Paint-your-own, crayons included | AED 59 | AED 89 |

Personalisation is where the real margin is. The plain tier exists to be undercut by
Amazon and to make the personalised option look obviously worth it.

### Why not AED 5

```
MDF material                      AED  3 to  6
laser time and labour             AED  5 to 10
packaging                         AED  3 to  5
                                  ─────────────
cost per unit before delivery     AED 11 to 21
```

At AED 5 you lose AED 6 to 16 per unit before anyone delivers anything. With delivery the
loss doubles. There is no volume that fixes a negative unit margin; more sales lose more
money.

## 4. Delivery

Three options at checkout, as requested.

| Option | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Workshop pickup | Free | Strongest margin. Promote it. |
| UAE delivery | AED 20 to 25 | Free above AED 200 to push basket size |
| International | Calculated | Quote per destination, longer lead time, customs on the buyer |

Pickup being free and prominent does two things: it protects margin on small orders, and
it gives price-sensitive customers a way to say yes without you discounting the product.

## 5. Policies (made to order)

- **Returns.** Made-to-order items are not returnable unless faulty. This must be stated
  on the product page before checkout, not buried in a policy page. UAE consumer
  protection expects pre-purchase disclosure.
- **Faulty or damaged.** Free replacement within **7 days**. This is what earns trust when
  returns are restricted. Make it prominent.
- **Personalised items.** Never returnable except faulty. Confirm spelling on screen
  before payment; a typo the customer supplied is not a fault.
- **Production time.** "Made to order in 2 to 3 working days" on every product page.
- **Clearance.** Different rules and say so clearly: stock items, returnable 7 days,
  unused, original packaging.
- **50/50 deposit.** Now makes more sense than it did for dropshipping. It protects you
  against a customer abandoning a piece you have already cut.

| Edge | AWS Lightsail Mumbai, Caddy, SSH reverse tunnel | Live, Let's Encrypt to 30 Oct |
| Cron | supercronic | Nightly inventory snapshot armed |

Deliberate proprietary edges: Stripe (no OSS settles cards), AWS SES behind Postal
(residential IP cannot deliver mail), Lightsail (needs a public IP), WhatsApp (Meta).

### What the pivot makes obsolete

- CJ Dropshipping integration, the sourcing agent, the landed-cost margin gate, the
  inventory-velocity intelligence. All of it modelled a supplier you no longer use.
  Keep the code, stop running it.
- `/api/variants` MDF matrices, `/api/import`, `data/cj-products.json`.
- The 50/50 split remains useful but for a different reason (see policies).

### Design direction

The current storefront was built for 515 cheap items: dense grids, small cards, price-led.
That is wrong for 41 handmade pieces. What it needs:

- Large photography. The images are the product. Full-bleed hero, generous white space.
- Warm neutral palette. The existing gold `#C9A96E` on off-white already suits the wood.
- Story above spec. Who made it, where, how long it takes.
- Age filters, not price filters. Parents shop by child age.
- Personalisation input on the product page, visible before add-to-cart.
- Trust markers: made in UAE, 7-day replacement, real workshop photos.

## 9. Open questions

1. **Personalisation.** Can the laser engrave names reliably at volume? This is the
   pricing strategy's foundation. If not, the premium tiers need a different justification.
2. **Capacity.** How many units per day realistically? It caps revenue and it determines
   whether a delivery promise of 2 to 3 days is safe.
3. **Material.** Is it plain MDF, birch-faced ply, or both? Affects copy and price.
4. **Sizes.** Needed per product for the spec line and for shipping quotes.
5. **Clearance stock.** What is it, how many, images to follow.
6. **Safety.** UAE toy standards and age labelling need checking before selling children's
   products at scale. Worth a conversation with a compliance advisor.
