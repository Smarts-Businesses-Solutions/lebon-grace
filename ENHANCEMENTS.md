## Product

### Order tracking that does not need a phone number
**Why:** The current credential is a phone plus an order id or email, and a hit
returns name, email, phone and address. A signed link in the confirmation email
would be both easier for the customer and tighter.
**Effort:** M · **Depends on:** nothing · **Source:** `docs/QA/ACTORS.md`

### Photograph-led review submission
**Why:** Reviews are tied to real delivered orders, which makes them credible.
Photographs of the actual piece in someone's home would compound that.
**Effort:** M · **Depends on:** image storage decision · **Source:** A-18 follow-on

### Gift mode
**Why:** Personalised children's puzzles are bought as gifts. A gift note and a
"do not include the price" flag are small and obvious.
**Effort:** S · **Depends on:** nothing · **Source:** product observation

### Made-to-order lead time on the product page
**Why:** The shop says "made one at a time" but never says *how long*. The
cutting queue already knows.
**Effort:** S · **Depends on:** A-15 queue data · **Source:** FAQ gap

## Platform

because it needs a real order to exist.
**Effort:** S · **Depends on:** nothing · **Source:** `USER_ACTIONS_INVENTORY.md`
